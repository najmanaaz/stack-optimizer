NORDLICHT ANALYTICS S.A R.L.
SECURITY AND DATA PROTECTION POLICY (SUMMARY)
Version 2.0 - Owner: Security lead - Reviewed: June 2026
Fictional document for testing the T4Tangerine Stack Optimizer.

1. CUSTOMER DATA

1.1 Customer data means any data our customers upload or that we generate about their deliveries, plus their users' names and emails.
1.2 Customer data must be stored and processed only in data centres inside the European Union. Backups too.
1.3 Approved providers for customer data: Amazon Web Services (EU regions only) and Microsoft Azure (EU regions only).
1.4 Any other vendor that would receive customer data needs a data processing agreement (DPA), an EU data location option, and approval from the Security lead.
1.5 AI services: customer data may only be sent to AI models hosted in the EU, and the provider must not use our data for training.

2. INTERNAL TOOLS (NO CUSTOMER DATA)

2.1 Internal tools (chat, documents, design, video calls) may be outside the EU if they offer a DPA.
2.2 Customer data must never be pasted into internal tools such as Slack, Notion, Miro or ChatGPT. Our current use of ChatGPT Team is limited to marketing and internal writing.

3. ACCESS

3.1 Every tool used by more than 5 people must use single sign-on (SSO) through Google Workspace.
3.2 Two-step verification is required on every account.
3.3 Access is removed within 24 hours when someone leaves.

4. LOGS AND MONITORING

4.1 Logs that may contain customer data are customer data (see section 1).
4.2 Logs are kept for at least 30 days and at most 13 months.

5. AVAILABILITY

5.1 The customer app must have a standby database in a second data centre.
5.2 Backups are tested every quarter.

6. CERTIFICATION

6.1 This policy will be extended to meet ISO 27001 in 2027.
