# SOP-07: Buying, renewing and cancelling software

**Owner:** Head of Finance · **Applies to:** everyone who buys software or cloud services · **Version:** 3.1, March 2026

*Fictional document for testing the T4Tangerine Stack Optimizer.*

## 1. Purpose

Keep our software spend under control, avoid surprise renewals, and make sure every tool that handles customer data is approved by Security.

## 2. Before buying a new tool

1. Check the tool list in Notion. If we already pay for a tool that does the job, use it.
2. Write a one-paragraph request in #purchasing: what problem it solves, who will use it, the monthly cost, and whether it will hold customer data.
3. Approvals:
   - Up to €200 per month: your team lead.
   - €200 to €1,000 per month: Head of Finance.
   - Over €1,000 per month, or any tool that holds customer data: CFO and Security lead.
4. Tools must support single sign-on (SSO) through Google Workspace if more than 5 people will use them.

## 3. Paying

- Pay by invoice where possible. The company card is only for tools under €200 per month.
- Choose yearly billing when it saves at least 15% and we are confident we will use the tool for 12 months.

## 4. Seat reviews

- Every quarter, each tool owner removes seats for people who left or have not logged in for 60 days.
- New starters get Slack, Google Workspace, Notion and 1Password by default. Everything else on request.

## 5. Renewals and cancellations

- Every contract has an owner and a renewal date in the tool list.
- Finance sends a reminder **90 days** before each renewal. The owner decides: renew, downgrade, switch or cancel.
- Known contract dates for next year:

## 6. Leaving a tool

Export our data, remove all user accounts, and delete the payment method before the renewal date.
