# Nordlicht Analytics: company goals for 2027

*Approved by the leadership team, September 2026. Fictional document for testing the T4Tangerine Stack Optimizer.*

## Where we are

- 120 business customers, €3.1 million in yearly revenue, growing about 40% a year.
- 32 people today; we plan to be **45 by the end of 2027** (8 more engineers, 3 sales, 2 customer success).
- Technology costs (software and cloud) are about **€67,000 a year**, roughly 2.2% of revenue and rising faster than revenue.

## Goals

### 1. Grow without growing costs at the same speed
- Cut technology spend by **20%** compared with today, before the January renewals.
- Keep technology costs **below 2% of revenue** for the whole year, even as we hire.
- Every tool has an owner and a reason to exist. Fewer vendors, fewer bills.

### 2. Win larger customers
- Pass an **ISO 27001** certification audit by **September 2027**. Large logistics firms require it.
- Offer customers a written guarantee that their data never leaves the EU.
- Reach **99.95% uptime** on the customer app, up from 99.9%, by the end of the year.

### 3. Move our product to one cloud
- Move the customer app and database from Heroku to **AWS in Frankfurt** by **June 2027**, with no more than one hour of planned downtime.
- Use managed AWS services so our engineers spend their time on the product, not on servers.

### 4. Use AI responsibly
- Add AI-written forecast explanations for all customers, keeping AI costs below **€1 per customer per month**.
- Customer data sent to AI services must stay in the EU.

## How we will measure progress

| Measure | Today | Target end of 2027 |
|---|---|---|
| Technology spend per year | about €67,000 | €53,000 or less |
| Technology spend as % of revenue | 2.2% | below 2% |
| Number of software vendors | 21 | 15 or fewer |
| Uptime | 99.9% | 99.95% |
| ISO 27001 | not started | certified |
