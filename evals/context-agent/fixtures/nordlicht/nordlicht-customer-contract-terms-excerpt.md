# Standard customer contract: service terms (excerpt)

*Applies to all Nordlicht Analytics customer contracts signed from January 2026. Fictional document for testing the T4Tangerine Stack Optimizer.*

## Service availability

- Nordlicht commits to **99.9% monthly availability** of the customer app, excluding announced maintenance.
- Planned maintenance is announced at least 5 days in advance and limited to 4 hours per month.
- If availability falls below 99.9% in a month, the customer receives a 10% service credit; below 99.0%, a 25% credit.

## Data processing

- Customer data is processed only in the European Union.
- Current sub-processors that receive customer data: Heroku (EU region), Auth0 (EU tenant), OpenAI (API, zero data retention).
- Nordlicht notifies customers 30 days before adding or changing a sub-processor.

## Support

- Support hours: Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 18:00 CET.
- Critical incidents: first response within 1 hour, 24/7.
